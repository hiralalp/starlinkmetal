Industrial Valves

We supply industrial valves for oil and gas, chemical, power, water and process plants. Our range covers ball, gate, globe, check (non-return), butterfly and needle valves in stainless steel, carbon steel and alloy bodies.

A valve is the control point of any piping system. It starts, stops, directs, regulates or protects the flow of liquids, gases, steam and slurries. Choosing the right valve type, body material and pressure class decides how safely and how long a line will run, so it pays to get this decision right at the start.

Starlink Metal Industries supplies the six valve families used most often in process plants, pipelines, utilities and building services: ball, gate, globe, check (non-return), butterfly and needle valves. Each family has its own strengths, and most plants use a mix of them on the same line.

As a rule of thumb, ball, gate and butterfly valves are used for on/off isolation, globe and needle valves are used where the flow must be adjusted, and check valves are fitted wherever reverse flow could damage a pump or contaminate a process. Use the pages in the Valves menu for the details of each type.

Valve Range

Ball Valves Quarter-turn shut-off. Floating and trunnion mounted, 2-piece and 3-piece, full or reduced bore.
Gate Valves Full-bore isolation for on/off service. Wedge type, rising or non-rising stem.
Globe Valves Throttling and flow regulation. Straight, angle and Y-pattern.
Check Valves (NRV) Prevent reverse flow. Swing, lift, wafer, dual plate and piston types.
Butterfly Valves Compact, light-weight shut-off and regulation. Wafer, lug and flanged.
Needle Valves Fine flow control and instrument isolation. Threaded and socket weld ends.

Body Materials & Connections

Body Materials Stainless steel CF8 / CF8M (304 / 316), carbon steel WCB, low-temperature LCB, duplex, alloy 20 and other alloys on request
End Connections Flanged (ASME B16.5), threaded (NPT / BSP), socket weld, butt weld, wafer and lug
Pressure Class Class 150 to Class 2500 depending on valve type
Design Standards API 600, API 602, API 608, API 6D, API 594, API 609, ASME B16.34

Selection Guide

Need quick on/off isolationBall valve (quarter-turn) or butterfly valve for larger, lower-pressure lines
Need full-bore isolation with minimum pressure dropGate valve, kept either fully open or fully closed
Need to throttle or regulate flowGlobe valve for line sizes, needle valve for small bore and instruments
Need to stop reverse flowCheck valve (swing, lift, wafer or dual plate)
Hygienic or food-grade serviceStainless steel sanitary butterfly, ball and angle valves with clamp or DIN ends
Corrosive or aggressive mediaStainless steel, duplex or alloy bodies with PTFE or alloy trim

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right valve?

Start with the job (on/off, regulating or non-return), then match the media, temperature, pressure and line size. Send us these details and we will suggest a suitable type, body material and pressure class.

What information do I need to send for a quotation?

Valve type, size, pressure class or rating, body and trim material, end connection, operation (lever, gear or actuator) and quantity.

Can valves be supplied with test certificates?

Yes. Material certificates and, where applicable, pressure test reports can be arranged on request. Please mention this in your enquiry.

Do you supply actuated valves?

Yes. We can supply valves with gear boxes, pneumatic actuators or electric actuators to suit your control system.
