Copper Technical Terms

Copper is a highly versatile metal known for its excellent electrical and thermal conductivity, along with strong corrosion resistance. It comes in various grades, each tailored for specific needs like strength, durability, and conductivity. Copper products include sheets, plates, rods, tubes, and fittings, serving a wide range of industries.
