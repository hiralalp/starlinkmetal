Globe Valves

Globe valves are used where flow needs to be regulated or throttled as well as shut off. We supply straight, angle and Y-pattern globe valves in cast and forged construction.

A globe valve has a movable disc or plug that presses against a seat set in the flow path. Because the disc moves directly toward and away from the seat, the amount of flow changes steadily as the stem is turned. This makes globe valves the standard choice where the flow has to be regulated or throttled as well as shut off.

The flow takes an S-shaped path through the body, so a globe valve has a higher pressure drop than a gate or ball valve. That resistance is the price of good control. When the valve is closed, the seat gives a tight seal, and the short stroke means wear on the seat is low and maintenance is easy.

Straight-pattern globe valves are the most common, angle-pattern valves turn the flow through 90 degrees and save an elbow, and Y-pattern valves have a straighter flow path with lower pressure drop. Flow direction normally goes under the disc, as marked by the arrow on the body.

We supply globe valves in cast and forged construction, in carbon steel, stainless steel and alloy materials, from small instrument sizes up to larger line sizes, with bolted, union or pressure seal bonnets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a globe valve have a higher pressure drop?

The flow has to change direction inside the body, which creates resistance. This resistance is what gives globe valves their good control.

Which way should a globe valve be fitted?

Follow the flow arrow on the body. Flow normally enters under the disc so that the stem packing is not under pressure when the valve is closed.

Is a globe valve better than a gate valve for steam?

Both are used. Globe valves are preferred where regulation or frequent operation is needed, and gate valves where only isolation with low pressure drop is needed.

Do you supply angle globe valves?

Yes. Angle-pattern globe valves are available in forged and cast designs, and in sanitary clamp-end designs.
