Alloy Steel & Chrome Moly

Chrome-moly alloy steels keep their strength at high temperature and resist creep and oxidation, making them standard for power plant, refinery and boiler piping.

Chrome-moly steel is low-alloy steel with added chromium and molybdenum. Chromium gives resistance to oxidation and corrosion at high temperature, while molybdenum gives strength and resistance to creep, the slow stretching that metals suffer under load at high temperature. Together they allow pipes to work at temperatures where plain carbon steel would weaken.

ASTM A335 is the specification for seamless ferritic alloy steel pipe for high-temperature service. Grade P11 has 1.25 percent chromium and P22 has 2.25 percent, and both are the workhorses of power plants and refineries. P5 and P9 have 5 and 9 percent chromium for better resistance to oxidation and sulphur. P91 and P92 are advanced 9 percent chromium steels that give much higher strength, so thinner pipe walls can be used in boilers and steam lines.

Matching forged flanges and fittings are made to ASTM A182 in grades F11, F22 and F91. These materials are usually welded with preheat and post-weld heat treatment, so proper procedures are essential.

ASTM A335 Pipe Grades

P1 Carbon-molybdenum steel (C-0.5Mo).
P5 5% chromium, 0.5% molybdenum.
P9 9% chromium, 1% molybdenum.
P11 1.25% chromium, 0.5% molybdenum.
P12 1% chromium, 0.5% molybdenum.
P22 2.25% chromium, 1% molybdenum.
P91 9% chromium, 1% molybdenum with vanadium and niobium. High creep strength.
P92 9% chromium with tungsten. Higher strength for ultra-supercritical plant.

Forgings & Fittings

ASTM A182 F11 Forged flanges and fittings, 1.25Cr-0.5Mo.
ASTM A182 F22 Forged flanges and fittings, 2.25Cr-1Mo.
ASTM A182 F91 Forged flanges and fittings, 9Cr-1Mo-V.
Applications Power plants, refineries, boilers, superheaters and high-temperature piping

Selection Guide

Moderate-temperature steam and refinery linesA335 P11 or P12
Higher temperature power pipingA335 P22
Oxidation and sulphur resistanceA335 P5 or P9
Ultra-supercritical boilers and steam linesA335 P91 or P92
Forged flanges and fittingsA182 F11, F22 or F91
Carbon-moly steel for lower temperaturesA335 P1

Frequently Asked Questions

What does P22 mean?

P stands for pipe, and 22 is the grade number. P22 has about 2.25 percent chromium and 1 percent molybdenum.

Why is P91 used in modern power plants?

P91 has high creep strength, so it allows thinner walls and higher steam temperatures and pressures.

Does chrome-moly steel need heat treatment after welding?

Usually yes. Preheat and post-weld heat treatment are normally required.

Which flange grade matches P22 pipe?

ASTM A182 F22 is the matching forged grade.
