Sanitary Bends & Tees

Sanitary bends and tees are the main direction-change and branch fittings on hygienic process lines. We supply polished stainless steel bends, tees, reducers and crosses with weld, union or clamp ends.

Bends and tees are the fittings that change direction or add a branch to a sanitary pipeline. In hygienic service they must not trap product, so their inside surfaces are smooth, their welds are ground flush and their radii are generous enough to avoid dead spots.

Bends are made in 45, 90 and 180 degree angles, in long radius for smoother flow and in short radius where space is limited. Tees are available as equal tees, reducing tees, crosses and Y-tees. Ends can be plain for welding, or machined for DIN or SMS unions, or fitted with TC ferrules for clamp connections.

Stainless steel 304 is common for dairy and food, and 316L is used for pharmaceutical, brewery and chloride-bearing service. The surface finish can be a satin or a mirror polish, depending on the hygiene requirement.

We supply sanitary bends and tees in a full range of sizes and end types, and can also supply fittings to your drawing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between long and short radius bends?

A long-radius bend has a larger bend radius, so flow is smoother and pressure drop is lower. A short-radius bend is more compact but has more resistance.

Can bends and tees be supplied with TC ends?

Yes. They can be supplied with weld ends, DIN or SMS union ends, or TC clamp ends.

What finish do you supply?

Satin and mirror polished finishes are available, to suit your hygiene requirement.
