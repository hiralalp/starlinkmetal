Our Speciality

U-Bolt Manufacturer

We manufacture U-bolts in-house — formed, threaded and finished to your drawing or sample. Used to clamp pipes, tubes, poles, cables and structural sections, our U-bolts are supplied with nuts, washers and mounting plates as required.

  • Round, square and semi-round (D-type) shapes
  • Mild steel, stainless steel and galvanised options
  • Zinc plated, hot-dip galvanised or plain finish
  • Custom diameters, lengths, thread pitch and quantities
  • Supplied with nuts, washers and saddle plates
