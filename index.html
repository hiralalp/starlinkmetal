About us

A Girgaon-based stockist that keeps it simple.

Starlink Metal Industries is a stockist and supplier of stainless steel pipes, tubes, flanges, fittings and industrial raw materials, based in Kumbharwada, Mumbai. Quick reference

Choosing a stainless steel grade

A short guide to the grades we are most often asked for. For full composition tables see the resources pages.

GradeCharacterTypical use
304General-purpose austeniticFood equipment, tanks, architectural, general piping
316 / 316LMolybdenum-bearing, better corrosion resistance; “L” is low-carbon for weldingChemical, marine and pharmaceutical lines
321Titanium-stabilised for heatHigh-temperature piping and exhaust systems
904LHigh-alloy austeniticAcidic and aggressive chemical service
DuplexMixed austenitic-ferritic, high strengthOil & gas, offshore and pressure applications
