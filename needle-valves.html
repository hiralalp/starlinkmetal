Needle Valves

Needle valves give fine, precise flow control and are widely used for instrumentation, sampling and gauge isolation. We supply forged and bar-stock needle valves in stainless steel and carbon steel.

Needle Valve Specifications

Types Forged needle valve, bar-stock instrumentation needle valve, angle pattern
Body Material A105, F304, F316, F316L, alloy on request
End Connections Threaded NPT / BSP, socket weld, compression ends
Pressure Rating Up to 6000 PSI depending on size and material
Stem Fine-thread stem with needle tip for accurate flow control
Standards ASME B16.34, MSS SP-99, API 602

Sampling & Instrument Valves

Use Sampling points, gauge isolation and instrument manifolds
Materials Stainless steel 304 / 316 and special alloys on request

Specifications shown are indicative. Please send us your grade, size, schedule/pressure class and quantity and we will confirm availability and a quotation.
