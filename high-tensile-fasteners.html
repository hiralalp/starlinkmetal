High-Tensile Fasteners

High-tensile fasteners carry heavy loads in structures, machinery, flanged joints and pressure equipment. We supply bolts, studs, nuts and washers in high-strength carbon and alloy steel as well as stainless steel.

High-tensile fasteners are bolts, studs and nuts made from high-strength steel, so they can clamp heavier loads with smaller diameters. They are used wherever a joint carries heavy loads, vibration or pressure: in structures, machinery, pressure vessels, flanged piping and heavy equipment.

In metric fasteners, strength is shown by a property class. A bolt marked 8.8 has a tensile strength of about 800 MPa, a 10.9 bolt about 1000 MPa and a 12.9 bolt about 1200 MPa. Nuts are marked 8, 10 or 12 to match. In ASTM specifications, A193 B7 is the common alloy steel stud bolt for high-temperature and high-pressure flanges, paired with A194 2H heavy hex nuts, while A193 B8 and B8M are stainless steel equivalents. A320 L7 is used for low-temperature service, and A325 and A490 are used for structural steel connections.

Coatings protect the fasteners from corrosion. Zinc plating is common for indoor use, hot dip galvanising for outdoor structures, and PTFE or Xylan coating for flange studs in corrosive or high-temperature service.

We supply hex bolts, stud bolts, full threaded studs, heavy hex nuts, washers and socket head screws in high-tensile steel and in stainless steel, in metric and inch threads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 8.8 or 10.9 mean on a bolt?

It is the property class. The first number is one hundredth of the minimum tensile strength in MPa, and the second shows the ratio of yield to tensile strength.

What is an A193 B7 stud?

A chromium-molybdenum alloy steel stud bolt widely used for high-temperature and high-pressure flange joints, normally used with A194 2H heavy hex nuts.

Which coating should I choose?

Zinc plating for general indoor use, hot dip galvanising for outdoor structures and PTFE or Xylan coating for flange studs in corrosive service.

Can I order fasteners to a drawing?

Yes, special sizes and lengths can be arranged. Send your drawing or specification.
