SMS / DIN Sanitary Fittings

SMS and DIN sanitary fittings are used on dairy, beverage, food and pharmaceutical lines where hygiene and easy cleaning matter. We supply bends, tees, reducers, unions and ferrules in polished stainless steel.

Sanitary fittings are designed for lines where cleanliness is as important as flow. Their inside surfaces are smooth, free of crevices and easy to clean, so milk, juice, beer and pharmaceutical products do not stick or harbour bacteria. SMS and DIN are two of the most common standards for these fittings.

SMS (Swedish standard SMS 1145) and DIN 11851 fittings use a nut and a slotted or threaded part that compress a seal between two machined ends. They can be taken apart quickly for cleaning and inspection, then put back together without welding. Weld ends are used for permanent runs, while union ends are used where lines need to be opened often.

Stainless steel 304 is used for general food and dairy service, while 316L gives better resistance to chlorides and cleaning chemicals, and is preferred for pharmaceutical and brewery lines. The inside surface is polished to a smooth finish, and welds are ground and polished to keep the line hygienic.

We supply a full range of SMS and DIN fittings, including bends, tees, reducers, unions, liners, nuts and caps, in polished stainless steel and in sizes to suit most process lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SMS and DIN fittings?

They are two different standards with slightly different dimensions and thread forms, so the parts are not interchangeable. Choose the same standard on both sides of a joint.

Should I choose 304 or 316L?

304 is fine for most dairy and food service. 316L is better where chlorides, aggressive cleaning chemicals or pharmaceutical purity are involved.

Can you supply fittings to a custom drawing?

Yes, on request. Send us the drawing and quantity.

Do you supply the seals as well?

Yes. Gaskets and seals in EPDM, silicone, NBR, Viton and PTFE can be supplied with the fittings.
