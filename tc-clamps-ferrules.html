Triclover (TC) Clamps & Ferrules

Triclover (TC) clamp fittings allow quick assembly and dismantling for cleaning on hygienic lines. We supply TC clamps, ferrules, end caps and related clamp-type fittings in stainless steel.

Triclover or tri-clamp (TC) fittings join two flat-faced ferrules with a gasket between them, held together by a single hinged clamp. A joint can be assembled or taken apart in seconds without any tools other than a wing nut or a wrench, which makes TC fittings very popular on hygienic process lines.

The parts of a TC connection are the ferrule (the flanged end that is welded or threaded to the pipe), the gasket and the clamp. Ferrules are made in short and long forms, and in blank, hose adapter and reducing versions. Clamps are single-pin, double-pin or heavy-duty, and the choice depends on the pressure and size.

TC fittings are made to ISO 2852 and DIN 32676 dimensions in sizes from small tubing up to large process pipe. Because the joint has no pockets where product can collect, and the gasket sits flush with the bore, it is easy to clean and suits CIP and SIP (clean-in-place and sterilise-in-place) systems.

We supply TC clamps, ferrules, end caps, elbows, tees, reducers and sight glasses in stainless steel 304 and 316L, along with matching gaskets in EPDM, silicone, NBR and PTFE.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a ferrule?

A ferrule is the flat-faced, flanged end that is welded to a pipe or tube. Two ferrules with a gasket between them are held together with a clamp to make the joint.

Are all tri-clamp sizes compatible?

Ferrules and clamps are made for specific nominal sizes. Choose clamps and gaskets of the same size and standard as the ferrules.

Which gasket should I use?

EPDM suits steam and caustic cleaning, silicone suits general food use and PTFE suits aggressive chemicals. Send us your media and temperature if you are unsure.

Can you supply complete assemblies?

Yes. We can supply ferrules, gaskets and clamps together, and fittings made to your drawing on request.
